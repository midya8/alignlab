04 / Limits and reproduction

Identification is not estimation.

The comparison probabilities here are exact population inputs under a Bradley-Terry scalar-reward model. An inconsistent cycle rejects that model for those exact inputs; noisy empirical rates can be inconsistent without disproving an underlying population model.

Undirected comparison-support connectivity determines population merits up to a global scale. It does not guarantee a finite-sample maximum-likelihood estimate. That is a different directed win-graph condition. This inspector performs no MLE fitting or smoothing.

Without scale bounds, any strictly positive component-mass vector is possible. Zero mass is a limit, not attained under positive finite merits. Full-support reference probabilities are required. General beta, confidence intervals, statistical uncertainty and neural training are outside this exact rational control.
